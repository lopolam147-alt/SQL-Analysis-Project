📌 Key Insights for 2025 (New Artist Strategy)

  • Release Timing: Autumn (August-October) shows the highest popularity, making it the optimal season to release new music.
  • Collaborations vs. Solos: Collaborations are more frequent among top artists. For new artists, collaborating with established names can boost visibility, provided they already have some baseline popularity.
  • Tempo: Avoid extremely slow or fast tempos --> stick to the normal range
  • Production Quality: High-popularity tracks are often recorded in quiet studios (low liveness) with high energy, suggesting a polished, immersive production style.
  • Genre Saturation: While pop, hip-hop, latin, gaming, and ambient dominate 2024, they are becoming saturated. Artists may find better opportunities in less crowded genres like rock, R&B, or punk, which had hits in 2024 but fewer releases in 2023-2024.
  • Artist Preference: Top artists in 2023-2024 lean heavily toward latin and hip-hop, increasing competition in these genres.
